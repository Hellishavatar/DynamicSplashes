# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial release, built from the Splash Ripples work on Water On Steroids: ripples that fade and
  scale with fall speed and movement, jump-out splashes, and the splashes the game skips on some
  landings. INI, no menu.
- Loads on Skyrim SE 1.5.97, AE 1.6.x and AE 1.7.99 / 1.7.104. With Water On Steroids installed,
  turn off its Splash Ripples so splashes don't get two sets of ripples.
